Frogger Game

This game is representation of the classic frogger game. Everytime you reach the top, you collect a star. You score after collectiong five starts. You start with 3 lives: every collision with an enemy costs one life and one star. Lose all lives and it's game over; reach a score of 5 and you win. Use the button on the bottom to select your character. Every level adds more enemies, and new enemy types appear as you level up. Step on the treasure chests that appear on the road to open a loot box and win a random reward, including extra lives (up to 6 hearts)! If you die, your level goes back to 1.
